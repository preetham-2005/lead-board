WEEK 10 STARTER PACK - OpenCode

1. Unzip this pack. Move the kanban-board folder somewhere easy, like Documents.
2. Open the kanban-board folder in the OpenCode desktop app.
3. Follow the Setup section of your Week 10 doc.

What is inside
- index.html ............ the starter app (a board that does nothing yet)
- openspec .............. where the plans and specs will live
- .agents/skills ......... OpenSpec commands, the Taste skill (minimalist-ui) and the Ponytail review skill
- AGENTS.md ............. Ponytail rules, always on

Everything here is plain text. Nothing in this pack runs on its own.
Third-party files and their licenses: THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md and the licenses folder.
